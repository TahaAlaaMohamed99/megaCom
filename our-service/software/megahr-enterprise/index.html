Complete Human Capital & Payroll Management for Enterprises

The design of the MegaHR Enterprise system has considered the following from its inception:

01

It was designed in accordance with laws, regulations, and systems applicable in Egypt, in addition to all standard HR functions available in International systems.

02

Implementation of all applicable rules and requirements from previous laws, along with current legal rules, while allowing for future legal amendments with minimal effort.

03

Simultaneous application of Civil Service Law and Private Sector Labor Law, enabling governmental and private sector entities to benefit from both systems in one application.

04

Pre-designed reports based on current legal requirements, with the ability to design custom reports as needed with minimal effort.
