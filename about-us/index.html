Megacom, Committed to Excellence

Megacom was established by mid-1997 as a shareholding company to provide consultation services. From the very beginning, Megacom has committed itself to "Excellence" in providing its services. In a world of rapid changes, only the best-organized companies would prevail.

As the need for change grows more and more every day, the need for high caliber expertise grows in the same manner. Megacom has engaged the activities of highly qualified professionals who possess proven track record of experience in each field it provides consultation for.

Through the past years of activities, Megacom has successfully fulfilled numerous assignments for companies and Organizations of different sizes and in different industries. It has conducted more than 300 consultation projects utilizing 180 Egyptian consultants of top caliber in addition to 70 of the best experienced European consultants.
